Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

BACK ("BACK", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how BACK handles information when you use the BACK mobile application (the "App").

Our goal is simple: BACK is designed to help you spend less time on your phone and more time in real life. We therefore aim to collect and use as little information as reasonably necessary to provide the App.

By using BACK, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

BACK is designed to minimize data collection.

Depending on the features you use, the App may process the following information.

1.1 Session and Life Data

When you use BACK, you may create information such as:

  • Session start and end times
  • Session duration
  • Selected activity
  • Custom activity names, icons, and colors
  • Moments and reflections
  • Feelings selected after a Session
  • Journal or reflection content that you choose to create
  • Life statistics derived from your Session history

This information is created by you through your use of the App.

1.2 Device Motion Information

BACK may use Apple's Core Motion framework to detect whether your device has been turned face down.

This functionality is used only to support the BACK Session experience, including the optional face-down confirmation and related haptic or voice feedback.

BACK does not use motion information to:

  • Track your physical location
  • Monitor your movements outside the App
  • Determine where you are
  • Build a personal profile
  • Sell or share your motion information with advertisers

Motion information used for this functionality is processed locally by the App and is not transmitted to BACK's servers.

1.3 App Preferences

BACK may store your App preferences, such as:

  • Light, Dark, or System theme preference
  • Voice preference
  • Haptic preference
  • Notification preference
  • Activity configuration
  • Other settings required to provide the App

1.4 iCloud Data

If you enable iCloud Sync, your BACK data may be stored and synchronized using Apple's iCloud services.

iCloud is an Apple service and is subject to Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

BACK does not receive or use your iCloud data for advertising or user profiling.

If you disable iCloud Sync, BACK will no longer synchronize your BACK data through iCloud.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

BACK does not intentionally collect:

  • Precise location information
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Camera content
  • Microphone recordings
  • Health data
  • Browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking
  • Your personal identity for advertising purposes

BACK does not require you to create an account to use the core functionality of the App.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by BACK is used only to provide and improve the functionality of the App.

For example, Session and Life data may be used to:

  • Record your Sessions
  • Display your Session history
  • Calculate Life statistics
  • Display Moments and Reflections
  • Restore your Activities and preferences
  • Synchronize your data through iCloud when enabled
  • Provide the App's core features

We do not use your personal data to build advertising profiles.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Storage

BACK is designed with a local-first approach.

Where applicable, your BACK data is stored locally on your device.

If you enable iCloud Sync, supported BACK data may also be synchronized through Apple's iCloud infrastructure.

BACK does not operate a separate user account system for the core App experience.

5. Data Sharing

BACK does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We do not share your Session history, Moments, Reflections, or other Life data with advertisers.

BACK may rely on Apple-provided services necessary to operate certain App functionality, including:

  • App Store services
  • In-App Purchase services
  • iCloud services, when enabled

Such services are governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

BACK does not use third-party advertising networks.

BACK does not intentionally transmit your personal Life data to third-party analytics or advertising services.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

BACK may offer optional paid features through Apple's App Store.

Payments and subscription transactions are processed by Apple through Apple's App Store and In-App Purchase systems.

BACK does not receive your full payment card information.

Information relating to your purchase or subscription may be provided to BACK by Apple as necessary to determine whether a subscription or purchase entitlement is active.

Your App Store purchases are also subject to Apple's applicable terms and policies.

7. Data Retention

BACK keeps your locally stored data for as long as you choose to keep it in the App.

You can delete your BACK data using the Delete Data function in Settings.

Deleting all BACK data removes your Session history, Moments, Reflections, Life data, and related user-generated content from the App.

Your Activity configuration and App preferences are not deleted when you use the Delete Data function.

If iCloud Sync is enabled, deletion is intended to apply to the corresponding synchronized BACK data as supported by the App.

8. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your BACK data directly from the App:

Settings → Delete Data → Delete All Data

You will be asked to confirm the deletion before it takes place.

Once deleted, your BACK data cannot be recovered through the App.

If you have questions about deletion or believe that additional information associated with you needs to be deleted, you may contact us using the contact information provided below.

9. Children's Privacy

BACK is not specifically directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by BACK.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because BACK is designed to minimize data collection and use local storage where possible, we aim to reduce the amount of personal information exposed to unnecessary systems.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the features you use, you may:

  • Choose whether to enable iCloud Sync
  • Choose whether to enable notifications
  • Choose whether to enable haptic feedback
  • Choose your voice preference
  • Delete your BACK data through Settings

You may also manage relevant device permissions through Apple's system Settings.

Your continued use of BACK after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective means that you acknowledge the updated policy.
